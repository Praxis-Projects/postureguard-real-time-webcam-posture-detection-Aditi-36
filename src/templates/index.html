Methodology

Behind the Mechanics

Our platform leverages local system pose extraction to establish dynamic vector baselines.

Forward Head Inclination

Estimates slope metrics mapping ears to shoulder midpoints. Threshold deviations over 15° initiate corrective alarm countdowns to minimize cervical spine loads.

Shoulder Slope Percentage

Computes height differences between left and right skeletal collar points relative to horizontal span. Slants exceeding 5% highlight unbalanced back strain distributions.

100% Secure & Local

PostureGuard runs fully on local host systems. No external databases, and no cloud-connected cameras. All computer-vision and pose computation loops operate in memory inside app.py and discard frames immediately after evaluation.
